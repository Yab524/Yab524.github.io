Responsibility Planning

RACI Matrix

Clarified who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for project activities.

R - ResponsibleCompletes the deliverable or task.
A - AccountableMakes final decisions and signs off on task completion. Only 1 per task.
C - ConsultedAn advisor, stakeholder, or subject matter expert who offers guidance before an action is taken.
I - InformedKept up to date on decisions made.
